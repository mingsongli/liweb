## Biography

I am a postdoctoral researcher at the School of Earth and Space Sciences, Peking University, supported by the Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship. I received my Ph.D. in Paleontology and Stratigraphy from Peking University in June 2026 under the supervision of Professor Mingsong Li. My research focuses on Triassic astrochronology, orbitally forced paleohydrological change, and Earth system modeling.

## Education

- Sep. 2021 – Jun. 2026, Ph.D. in Paleontology and Stratigraphy, Peking University. Supervisor: Mingsong Li.
- Oct. 2024 – Oct. 2025, Visiting Ph.D. Student, University of Leeds. Supervisors: Paul Wignall, Benjamin Mills, and Alan Haywood.
- Sep. 2017 – Jun. 2021, B.S. in Geology, Jilin University.

## Professional Experience

- Jul. 2026 – Present, Postdoctoral Researcher, Peking University (Boya Postdoctoral Fellowship).

## Research Interests

- Triassic cyclostratigraphy and astrochronology.
- Orbitally forced paleoclimate and paleohydrological change.
- Sea-level and groundwater changes in greenhouse periods.
- Earth system modeling of deep time climate and hydrological change.
- Time series analysis of geological and paleoclimate records.
