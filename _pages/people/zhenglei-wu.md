## Personal Profile

I am Wu Zhenglei, an undergraduate student at the School of Earth and Space Sciences, Peking University, under the supervision of Professor Mingsong Li. My research primarily focuses on cyclostratigraphy and the construction of astrochronological timescales. I am dedicated to establishing high-precision geological age frameworks by utilizing astronomical cyclic signals preserved in stratigraphic records, combined with time series analysis and astronomical tuning methods, thereby providing critical chronological support for studies on deep-time climate evolution and Earth's orbital forcing.
